Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

mmaximov content lab is a personal tool used only by its developer, Mikhail Maximov, to manage his own YouTube channel.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent the app uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube account of the signed-in user: channel and video metadata, comments, captions, and YouTube Analytics reports.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show channel statistics to the owner and to update the owner's own videos (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists).

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and any downloaded reports are stored on the owner's own computer. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens.
